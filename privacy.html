Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

ReportFlow turns tasks from Trello, Asana, ClickUp and monday.com into one dashboard and client-ready reports. It comes in two forms: the ReportFlow Chrome extension and the ReportFlow web app. They handle data differently, so this policy covers each one separately.

The short version. The Chrome extension runs entirely in your browser and has no server. The web app, if you use it, stores your account and your encrypted platform tokens on our server so it can sync in the background. In both, ReportFlow only reads your tasks. It never creates, edits or deletes anything in your boards, and we do not sell your data.

Part 1. Chrome extension

The extension runs inside your browser. There is no ReportFlow server involved.

Disconnecting a platform removes its token immediately, and uninstalling the extension removes all of its local data.

What we do with it

  • Read your tasks, using your tokens, only to display them to you and generate the exports you request.
  • We never create, change or delete anything in your Trello, Asana, ClickUp or monday.com accounts.
  • We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to train models.
  • We do not include third-party advertising or tracking scripts.

Service providers

The web app relies on a hosting provider to run the server and store the database, and on Stripe to process payments. These providers process data only to provide those services. Task data is also fetched from Trello, Asana, ClickUp and monday.com themselves, whose own privacy policies apply to data in those platforms. [Hosting provider name to be added before launch.]

Your controls

  • Remove a connection: deletes its encrypted token and its synced task data.
  • Delete your account: in Settings, one click permanently deletes your account, tokens, settings and synced data from the application database. Backups, if any, are overwritten on the normal backup cycle.
  • Revoke access at the source: you can revoke any token in the platform where you created it, which immediately stops ReportFlow reading from it.
  • Subscription: cancel any time from the billing portal.

Retention

We keep your account data while your account exists. If you delete it, the data described above is deleted. Sessions expire after 30 days. Billing records may be kept by Stripe and by us as required for tax and accounting.

Security

We use encryption for stored tokens, hashed passwords and session tokens, HTTPS in production, request protections against cross-site attacks and rate limiting on sign-in. No system is perfectly secure, and you should use a unique password and only create platform tokens with the access you are comfortable with.

Children

ReportFlow is intended for business use and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes to this policy

If our data handling changes, we will update this page and the date above.

What ReportFlow is

ReportFlow is a browser tool that reads task data from Trello, Asana, and/or ClickUp accounts you connect, and formats it into status reports you can copy, export, or send. It runs entirely in your browser and has no backend server. See the Privacy Policy for details on data handling.
